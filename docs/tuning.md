# Hyperparameter tuning (S08)

Generated by `scripts/tune/freeze.py` from `results/tuning/` and `results/tuning_summary.json`. Protocol: ANALYSIS_PLAN section 6 with the clarifications of `docs/deviations.md` (2026-10-07): one Optuna study per model and anchor, `TPESampler(seed=0)`, no pruning, 30 COMPLETE trials, seed-0 trials on resample 0's nested training hosts, one validation set per anchor shared by all four models, max_epochs 200 and patience 30. The test hosts of resample 0 were removed from memory in every tuning process.

The objective is the best validation MAE of a trial: E_f in eV for S, D-state and D-late; the mean absolute error over the 22 standardised descriptors (dimensionless) for P1.

## Selection of D

Rule (logged before tuning): lower mean over the anchors of the best-trial validation MAE (E_f, eV).

| Variant | a50 | a200 | a654 | Mean |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| D-state | 0.4188 | 0.2847 | 0.2178 | 0.3071 |
| D-late | 0.4027 | 0.3228 | 0.1960 | 0.3072 |

**D = D-state** (mean 0.3071 eV against 0.3072 eV for D-late; difference 0.0001 eV). `models.injection_mode` is set to `D-state` and its tuned values are copied under `D` in `configs/tuned_v1.yaml`. D-late is reported from its tuning runs only, in the SI (ANALYSIS_PLAN section 12d).

The margin, 0.000077 eV, is far below the top-5 spreads of the D studies (0.0050 to 0.0206 eV), and the variants win at different anchors (D-state at 200; D-late at 50, 654). The two variants are therefore indistinguishable on this objective. The logged rule is applied as written; the selection is not evidence that one injection route is better.

## Epoch cap

The cap of 200 epochs (frozen before tuning) stopped the best trial of S at anchor 654, P1 at anchor 50; the other best trials stopped early. The cap is part of the frozen protocol and was not changed; the number of trials per study that reached it is in the table above.
