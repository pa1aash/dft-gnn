# GPU cost table (S07)

Remaining pre-registered training workload on the measured card (L40S, 1 GPU, 44.4 GiB usable). Numbers are generated by `scripts/cost_table.py` from committed results (`results/cost_table.json`); nothing here is a new scientific result.

## Basis

- Epoch limits set by the logged rule: max_epochs 200, patience 30; e_conv per piloted budget {25: 30, 200: 70, 654: 133}.
- Seconds per epoch at 654 hosts (set2set, single worker): mean over the 18 benchmarked (hidden, batch, blocks) cells 2.44 s (central), slowest cell 4.56 s (upper); the mid-space cell (64, 32, 3) takes 1.55 s. Scaled to each budget by the measured epoch time (training plus the validation pass, `results/gpu_epoch_scaling.json`): 25: x0.049, 50: x0.079, 100: x0.213, 200: x0.361, 400: x0.665, 654: x1.073, kiyohara: x1.130. The cell of the tuned model is unknown until tuning, so the central value uses the grid mean for every stage and the upper bound the slowest cell.
- Central: expected epochs = e_conv(B) + patience (log-linear interpolation of e_conv between the piloted budgets); concurrency speedup 1.23x (CUDA MPS, 4 workers). Upper: slowest cell, max_epochs epochs, no concurrency gain.
- Price: L40S Secure Cloud $1.09/h GPU (RunPod public API) plus $0.04/h disks (observed) = $1.13/h.

Stage notes: tune: S, D-state, D-late, P1 x anchors x trials; one run per trial; sweep: S, D, P1 x resamples x budgets x seeds (P is evaluation only); kiyohara: S, D, P1 x seeds; loco: S, D x folds x seeds; training size taken as the 654-host maximum; sensitivity: high-moment exclusion, S and D at the maximum budget; cgcnn: S, D x budgets x seeds, resample 0; timed with the MEGNet-S cell (a CGCNN backbone was not benchmarked).
Excluded (not measured or not GPU work): MLIP relaxations (MACE-MP-0, S09+), latent probe (CPU), figures and statistics (CPU).

## Concurrency and saturation (current card only)

| Workers | Aggregate epochs/h, no MPS | Speedup vs 1 | Aggregate epochs/h, CUDA MPS | Speedup vs 1 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 2311 | 1.00 | 2334 | 1.00 |
| 2 | 1978 | 0.86 | 2671 | 1.14 |
| 4 | 1876 | 0.81 | 2882 | 1.23 |
| 8 | 1854 | 0.80 | 2935 | 1.26 |

**Saturation.** nvidia-smi utilisation was 93% with one worker and 99-100% with 2-8 workers; without MPS the aggregate rate fell to 0.86x (2 workers) and 0.80x (8 workers) of one worker's, because the processes time-slice the device. With CUDA MPS it rose to 1.23x at 4 workers and 1.26x at 8, so the card is saturated at about 4 workers. Utilisation did not stay low, so the data do not support moving to several cheaper cards to gain throughput; running one pod with MPS and about 4 workers is the efficient setting on this card.
