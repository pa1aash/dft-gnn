with the date, the plan section, the deviation, the reason and the impact. All deviations are reported in
the SI. Results generated before a deviation are retained and reported.

| 2026-10-08 | §7 | CLARIFICATION (does not alter any pre-registered decision). Hierarchical bootstrap. 2000 replicates (`analysis.bootstrap.draws`). In each replicate, 10 resamples are drawn with replacement from the 10; within each drawn resample its test hosts are drawn with replacement (host-level cluster: every site of a drawn host is included, a host drawn twice counts twice), the metric of each model and A are recomputed on that draw, and the replicate value is the mean over the 10 draws. One-sided 95% upper bound = 95th percentile of the 2000 replicate values; two-sided 95% interval = 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles (numpy `quantile`, default linear interpolation). Generator: `numpy.random.default_rng(20261008)`, one generator per analysis, drawing for each replicate and each of its 10 slots first the resample index (`integers`) and then the host multiplicities (`multinomial`). The same draws are applied to S, D and every baseline at every budget and for every metric (site MAE, within-host residual MAE, host-mean MAE), so all contrasts are paired.
