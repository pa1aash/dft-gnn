**Figure 2.** Readout of the structure-only model S and the D-state injection points. The inputs are the initial features of the vacancy node $\mathbf{x}_\mathrm{v}^{(0)}$ and of the other nodes $\mathbf{x}_i^{(0)}$, the edge features $\mathbf{e}_{ij}^{(0)}$ and the global state $u^{(0)}$; $n$ MEGNet blocks update them. The readout concatenates three vectors: the final embedding of the vacancy node $\mathbf{h}_\mathrm{v}$, a pooling of all node embeddings $\bar{\mathbf{h}}$ (Set2Set or mean, a tuned choice) and the final global state $u$. A multilayer perceptron maps the concatenation to the scalar formation energy $E_\mathrm{f}$. The physical motivation is that $E_\mathrm{f}$ separates into a host-level thermodynamic term, carried by $\bar{\mathbf{h}}$ and $u$, and a local-environment term, carried by $\mathbf{h}_\mathrm{v}$. In D (D-state), the host-electronic descriptors are appended to $u^{(0)}$ and the site-electronic descriptors to $\mathbf{x}_\mathrm{v}^{(0)}$ (green arrows), standardised with training-set statistics; the readout is unchanged.
