**Figure S2.** Split designs, drawn as sets of hosts (dark: test; light: training; mid-grey: validation); every split groups by formula, which in this database is the same as grouping by host, so no host contributes sites to both sides. Widths are schematic, not to scale. (**a**) Primary protocol: each of the outer resamples holds out 20% of the hosts as test hosts, stratified by host-mean $E_\mathrm{f}$, and orders the remaining pool so that the training set at budget $B$ is the first $B$ hosts; budgets are nested, and the learning curves are drawn against $B$. Validation hosts are carved out of each training set. (**b**) The published split of Kiyohara et al.: the graph networks train on its train hosts, early-stop on its validation hosts and are tested on its test hosts. (**c**) Grouped $K$-fold cross-validation ($K$ = 10 and 20; extension, not pre-registered): every host is tested exactly once, with training sets larger than the largest budget of (a). (**d**) Leave-one-chemistry-out: hosts are grouped into chemistry families (the set of periodic-table groups of the cations), and five folds, each holding out whole families, test transfer to chemistries absent from training.
