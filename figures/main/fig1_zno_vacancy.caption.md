**Figure 1. The neutral oxygen vacancy in wurtzite ZnO, as it enters the data set.** Pristine
PBEsol(+U)-relaxed ZnO supercell of the Kumagai et al. database (Phys. Rev. Materials 5, 123803, 2021; host
mp-2133, space group P6_3mc, 5 × 5 × 3 supercell, a = 15.876 Å, c = 15.348 Å, 300 atoms, Zn150O150). The
vacancy is created by removing oxygen atom 150 (irreducible site O1, Wyckoff b, site symmetry 3m); its
position is marked by the translucent amber sphere and the removed atom itself is not drawn. Its four
nearest Zn neighbours (1.934 Å three times and 1.942 Å, the tetrahedral coordination of wurtzite) are drawn
enlarged and joined into the translucent tetrahedron with dark edges. Zn grey-blue, O red; only Zn-O bonds shorter than
2.32 Å (1.2 × the shortest Zn-O distance) are drawn, and bonds that cross the supercell boundary are
omitted. The supercell is shifted by lattice translations only, so that the vacancy lies about 2.6 Å inside
the two cell faces nearest the viewer; the structure is the pristine host, not the relaxed defect cell
(in the relaxed neutral defect cell no atom moves more than 0.17 Å; `docs/data_audit.md`, section 5).
Orthographic view along (−0.46, −0.80, 0.37) in Cartesian coordinates (azimuth −120°, elevation 22°, c
axis up). Rendered with `blender/render_host.py mp-2133 150` (Blender 5.2.2, Cycles on CPU, 128 samples,
OpenImageDenoise); 3274 × 2591 px, 600 dpi at the 138.6 mm text width (frame fitted to the projected cell). Render parameters and the CIF
checksum: `figures/main/fig1_zno_vacancy.render.json`.
