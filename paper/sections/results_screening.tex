\subsection{Screening of named oxides}
\label{sec:screening}

A practical question follows: how close the structure-only network comes to the DFT value for oxides that a defect chemist would name, and to which of them it can be applied at all (claim C5). This is a demonstration, not a test. S is applied at \numScreeningDemoBudget{} training hosts to the named candidates of the analysis plan; every oxide shown was a test host in at least one outer resample, and the prediction for a site is the seed-ensemble mean averaged over the resamples in which its host was held out, the aggregation registered for v1. The v2 predictions are post hoc; both are shown.

The absences are a measured result about the domain. Of the \numScreeningDemoScopeNCandidates{} candidates, \numScreeningDemoScopeNPresent{} are in the universe, with \numScreeningDemoScopeNScreenedSites{} oxygen sites in \numScreeningDemoScopeNHosts{} hosts (Table~\ref{tab:screening_sites}). In$_2$O$_3$, CeO$_2$, WO$_3$, MoO$_3$, Nb$_2$O$_5$ and LaAlO$_3$ have no host of that formula in the release, which records no reason. SrTiO$_3$ and BaTiO$_3$ are in the release, but every neutral entry carries the perturbed-host-state flag (\numScreeningDemoScopePhsRemovedSrTiOThreePhsEntries{} of \numScreeningDemoScopePhsRemovedSrTiOThreeNeutralEntries{} and \numScreeningDemoScopePhsRemovedBaTiOThreePhsEntries{} of \numScreeningDemoScopePhsRemovedBaTiOThreeNeutralEntries{}), so the filter of Section~\ref{sec:domain} removes them, and TiO$_2$, whose release host is a monoclinic polymorph (space group number \numScreeningDemoHostsTiOTwoSpaceGroupNumber{}), keeps one site because \numScreeningDemoScopeTioTwoReleasePhsEntries{} of its \numScreeningDemoScopeTioTwoReleaseNeutralEntries{} neutral entries are flagged. These removals are consistent with the over-representation of oxides with a low-lying empty cation $d$ band among shallow-donor entries (Section~\ref{sec:domain}) but do not prove a mechanism; the demonstration covers only oxides whose neutral vacancy the release treats as a deep, localised defect.

Across oxides, both networks reproduce the coarse energy scale. The DFT energies fall into two groups (Figure~\ref{fig:screening}a), the sites of SnO$_2$, ZnO, Ga$_2$O$_3$ and TiO$_2$ lower and those of ZrO$_2$, MgO and Al$_2$O$_3$ higher, and both networks place every site in its group; the rank correlation of predicted with DFT energy, \numScreeningDemoSummaryVTwoSpearmanAcrossSites{} for v2 and \numScreeningDemoSummaryVOneSpearmanAcrossSites{} for v1, is dominated by that separation. A physical reading of the two groups is left to the Discussion.

Within a host, the networks order sites only where the coordination differs (Table~\ref{tab:screening_pairs}). In Ga$_2$O$_3$ both order all three pairs as DFT does, with the four-coordinate site O$_2$ highest, but for the two three-coordinate sites the predicted difference is a small fraction of the DFT difference and the per-resample ranges overlap. In ZrO$_2$ both networks invert the DFT order of the four-coordinate site O$_2$, which has the longer mean Zr--O distance, and the three-coordinate site O$_1$.

The largest error is \numScreeningDemoSummaryVTwoMaxAbsErrEV{}~eV for v2, at the O$_2$ site of ZrO$_2$, and \numScreeningDemoSummaryVOneMaxAbsErrEV{}~eV for v1, at the TiO$_2$ site. Al$_2$O$_3$, among the sites of highest DFT energy, is under-predicted by both networks, and its per-resample predictions on v2 (\numScreeningDemoSitesAlTwoOThreeVaOOneZeroVTwoMinEV{} to \numScreeningDemoSitesAlTwoOThreeVaOOneZeroVTwoMaxEV{}~eV, against \numScreeningDemoSitesAlTwoOThreeVaOOneZeroDftEV{}~eV) show that the error persists whichever training hosts are drawn.

Within the domain of the models, the structure-only network reproduces which of these oxides cost more energy to reduce, as two groups, and the order of \numScreeningDemoSummaryVTwoWithinHostPairsConcordant{} of the \numScreeningDemoSummaryVTwoWithinHostPairs{} within-host pairs. These sites neither establish nor exclude a ranking of sites finer than the error, and nothing was measured for the absent candidates.
