\section{Default-Initialisation Ablation}
\label{app:ablation}

The ablation keeps everything but the initial weights: the same architecture, data, splits, training seeds, seed ensembles and estimators, with the default initialisation of the library and settings tuned under it, against the network with He initialisation (Section~\ref{sec:training}). Table~\ref{tab:app_ablation} sets the two side by side at every training size and design, together with the within-host spread of the predictions, the standard deviation of the predictions over the sites of a test host divided by that of the DFT energies (mean over test hosts with at least two sites; mean within-host spread of the DFT energies \numNetSpreadTargetSd{}~eV). A spread ratio near zero means that a model predicts one energy per host.

The structure-only error of the network at \numNetSixHundredFiftyFourHosts{} training hosts, \numNetSixHundredFiftyFourMaeS{} [\numNetSixHundredFiftyFourMaeSLo{}, \numNetSixHundredFiftyFourMaeSHi{}]~eV, lies above that of the default initialisation, \numAblSixHundredFiftyFourMaeS{} [\numAblSixHundredFiftyFourMaeSLo{}, \numAblSixHundredFiftyFourMaeSHi{}]~eV, and the two intervals overlap. The He initialisation was chosen to resolve sites, not to lower the error: with the default initialisation S predicts one energy per host up to \numCfgBudgetsHostsItemFour{} training hosts (spread ratio at most \numAblSpreadRatioSFifty{}), whereas the network resolves sites from \numCfgBudgetsHostsItemThree{} hosts on (\numNetSpreadRatioSOneHundred{} and \numNetSpreadRatioSTwoHundred{} at \numCfgBudgetsHostsItemThree{} and \numCfgBudgetsHostsItemFour{} hosts), and both reach a similar spread with \numCfgBudgetsMax{} training hosts (\numNetSpreadRatioSSixHundredFiftyFour{} and \numAblSpreadRatioSSixHundredFiftyFour{}). The descriptor network spreads its predictions within a host at every size under both initialisations.

The descriptor advantage stays inside $\pm\delta$ under both configurations from \numCfgBudgetsMax{} training hosts on. With \numCfgBudgetsMax{} hosts, over the additional resamples, $A = \numNetSixHundredFiftyFourThirtyByFiveA{}$ [\numNetSixHundredFiftyFourThirtyByFiveALo{}, \numNetSixHundredFiftyFourThirtyByFiveAHi{}]~eV for the network and \numAblSixHundredFiftyFourThirtyByFiveA{} [\numAblSixHundredFiftyFourThirtyByFiveALo{}, \numAblSixHundredFiftyFourThirtyByFiveAHi{}]~eV for the default initialisation, and the two cross-validations keep both inside the tolerance. With \numCfgBudgetsHostsItemFive{} training hosts neither configuration is inside it. The two initialisations differ in the sign of $A$ at the largest sizes, which is negative for the default initialisation, and in the leave-one-family-out folds, where only the network exceeds $\delta$ (Section~\ref{sec:learning_curves}).

On the published split of Kiyohara et al.\ \citep{Kiyohara2025machinelearning}, with its own training, validation and test hosts (Appendix~\ref{app:splits}), S with the default initialisation reaches \numAblKiyoharaMaeS{} [\numAblKiyoharaMaeSLo{}, \numAblKiyoharaMaeSHi{}]~eV, against the published structure-only \numNetPublishedMae{}~eV. The descriptor injection route of D was chosen once, under the default initialisation, by the mean over the three tuning sizes of the best validation error: \numTuningSummaryDecisionMeansEVDState{}~eV for injection into the initial global state and vacancy-site features against \numTuningSummaryDecisionMeansEVDLate{}~eV for injection at the readout, a difference of \numTuningSummaryDecisionDifferenceEV{}~eV.
